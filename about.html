About Me

Telecommunications Engineering • Networks • Infrastructure

Summary

I am a Telecommunications Engineering student with a strong interest in the design, deployment and operation of reliable network infrastructures. Growing up in a rural area in Spain, I experienced first-hand how limited and unstable connectivity can affect access to education and essential services. These experiences shaped my motivation to understand how communication systems work in practice and how better network design can reduce inequalities of opportunity.

Background & Motivation

During the COVID-19 pandemic, unreliable Internet access at my home frequently disrupted online classes and collaborative work. I approached this as an engineering problem, identifying a nearby fibre-connected site and designing a point-to-point wireless link to extend high-speed connectivity to my home.

This project became my first real exposure to network design in operational conditions and highlighted the technical and social impact of communication infrastructure.

Academic Focus

Throughout my Bachelor’s degree in Telecommunication Technologies at Comillas Pontifical University, I developed a solid foundation in computer networks, signal processing and software engineering. I have been consistently drawn to understanding how theoretical principles behave in real systems.

This interest culminated in my Final Year Project, where I am designing and implementing a protocol stack for multihoming in IPv6 and mixed IPv4–IPv6 environments using adaptive address translation and packet encapsulation.

Technical Interests

My work has strengthened my interest in network reliability, resilience and large-scale architecture, as well as the role of open standards in ensuring interoperability and long-term stability. I am particularly interested in how protocol design choices influence real-world deployability and operational complexity.

Hands-On Experience

Outside formal coursework, I actively pursue hands-on projects. These include the deployment of wireless backhaul links, full local network installations with structured cabling, and the continuous development of a personal home lab used to experiment with virtualization, service deployment and system integration.

Outlook

In the long term, I aim to contribute to the development of resilient, efficient and scalable communication networks. This goal is closely tied to my own experience growing up in an under-connected environment, where the effects of poor infrastructure are immediate and tangible.
